4. Impurities, Traces, and Packaging Material Information

4.1 Ingredient Purity

All ingredients are supplied at 100% purity as declared by the respective suppliers on Safety Data Sheets. No known process impurities have been identified.

4.2 Heavy Metal Screening

No heavy metal contamination has been declared by ingredient suppliers. No ethoxylated ingredients (potential 1,4-dioxane source) are present. No formaldehyde releasers are used. The formulation does not contain nitrosamine precursor combinations.

Note: Magnesium Chloride is a naturally derived mineral ingredient; supplier certificates confirm pharmaceutical/technical grade with no heavy metal exceedances declared.

4.3 Packaging Compatibility

  • Container: HDPE (High Density Polyethylene) spray bottle.
  • HDPE is chemically inert and compatible with aqueous-based formulations at pH 4.8–5.5.
  • No plasticizer migration risk identified for this polymer type at the target pH.
  • Stability testing in HDPE packaging confirmed no packaging–product interaction throughout 12-week accelerated study.
  • No NIAS (Non-Intentionally Added Substances) risk identified.

3. Reasoning

3.1 Regulatory Compliance

All 12 ingredients have been verified against EU Annexes II through VI of Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009. No prohibited substances (Annex II) are present. No restricted substances (Annex III) are used above their permitted concentrations. Preservatives Sodium Benzoate (0.010%, max 0.5%) and Potassium Sorbate (0.010%, max 0.6%) are within Annex V limits. No Annex IV colorants, Annex VI UV filters, or Article 15 CMR Category 1A/1B substances are present.

3.2 Exposure Assessment

Systemic exposure was calculated using product-specific application data from the CPSR Entry Form (20/05/2026): DAa = 7,820 mg/day (7.82 g applied daily); 50% dermal absorption (SCCS default); 70 kg adult body weight. The formula SED = (C/100) × DAa × (DA/100) / BW yields a constant of 55.857 per percentage point.

3.3 Systemic Safety

All 11 systemically assessable ingredients (Aqua excluded) have MoS values above the minimum threshold of 100. The lowest MoS is 234 (Magnesium Chloride at 10% w/w), which remains 2.3-fold above the safety threshold. Exposure parameters are based on actual product use data (7.82 g/day) from the CPSR Entry Form dated 20/05/2026.

3.4 Local Effects

All ingredients are well tolerated at cosmetic use levels. The pH of 4.8–5.5 is physiologically appropriate for leave-on skin products (compatible with normal skin pH range 4.5–6.5). No ingredients are classified as skin sensitisers or corrosives at in-use concentrations. The product is formulated specifically for sensitive skin, with no synthetic fragrances and gentle humectants (Glycerin, Mel) included.

3.5 Microbiological Safety

The product passed both microbiological analysis (ISO 17516:2004, sample XW252) and preservation efficacy challenge testing (ISO 11930, Criteria A, sample XW252, 28 days). The preservation system (Sodium Benzoate + Potassium Sorbate + Sodium Anisate + Sodium Levulinate) achieves complete reduction of all test organisms within 7 days. Results were signed by qualified laboratory personnel at MySwissLab+.

3.6 Stability

Two independent stability studies were conducted: XRAEP (12 weeks, cold/ambient/heat, PET packaging, 05/04/2023) and XW252 (12 weeks, HDPE packaging, 25/09/2023). Both confirmed no variation in appearance, colour, odour, texture, or viscosity across all test conditions. The 12M PAO and 24-month unopened shelf life are supported by the stability data. The commercial product is packaged in HDPE, which is more chemically inert than PET; the stability data is therefore considered representative and conservative.
