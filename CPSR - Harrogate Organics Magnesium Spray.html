4. Impurities, Traces, and Packaging Material

All ingredients are declared at 100% purity by their respective suppliers on Safety Data Sheets. No prohibited substances listed in Annex II of Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009 have been identified in the formulation. No PBT, vPvB, CMR Category 1A/1B, or endocrine-disrupting substances are present above 0.1%.

1,4-Dioxane — Worst-Case Risk Assessment (PEG-40 Hydrogenated Castor Oil)

PEG-containing ingredients may carry trace levels of 1,4-dioxane as a by-product of the ethoxylation manufacturing process. A worst-case assessment has been performed in the absence of specific supplier COA data:

  • Assumed 1,4-dioxane in raw material: 20 ppm (conservative industry worst-case for cosmetic-grade PEG ingredients)
  • PEG-40 Hydrogenated Castor Oil at 1.0% w/w in finished product
  • 1,4-dioxane in finished product: 1.0% × 20 ppm = 0.20 ppm
  • SCCS leave-on limit for 1,4-dioxane: 1 ppm (SCCS/1539/14)

Conclusion: Finished product concentration (0.20 ppm) is 5× below the SCCS leave-on limit. The 1,4-dioxane risk is acceptable. Supplier COA confirming ≤20 ppm in raw material to be retained on file when available.

No formaldehyde releasers, nitrosamine precursor combinations, or ethoxylated ingredients other than PEG-40 HCO are present. No nanomaterials declared. No heavy metal contamination declared by any ingredient supplier.

5.1 Directions for Use (as labelled)

Label Directions

“We recommend applying 2–3 sprays inside each elbow and also behind the knees (where the skin is thinner) for a great night’s sleep. For headaches and migraines, simply spray on your palms and massage into your temples and the back of your neck. You can also apply to any aching joints or sore muscles for pain relief. Simply spray onto these tender areas and massage deep into the skin. 1 spray = Approx 18 mg of magnesium, so we recommend between 10–15 sprays for adults.”

5.2 Reasonably Foreseeable Misuse

  • Eye contact: Accidental eye contact during application near the face (temples, neck area). Label warning to avoid eye contact recommended.
  • Children: Product is labelled for adults only; not assessed for children under 3 years.
  • Broken skin: Application to broken or damaged skin not specifically restricted in directions; assessor may wish to add label caution.
  • Inhalation: Inhalation of spray mist during application is a relevant secondary exposure route (addressed in Section 6).
  • Ingestion: Accidental ingestion unlikely given spray format; label “for external use only” applies.

6.2 Inhalation Screening

This product is delivered via a spray bottle, making inhalation of aerosol mist during application a relevant secondary exposure route. However, this is a non-volatile aqueous solution; no volatile organic solvents are present. Eucalyptus Globulus Leaf Oil (0.009% w/w) is volatile and fragrant, but is present at an extremely low concentration in the formula.

Under normal use conditions (short-duration spraying of a water-based product), the risk of significant systemic inhalation exposure is considered low. A formal quantitative inhalation risk assessment has not been performed. The assessor should confirm whether one is required, particularly for the eucalyptus oil component.

7. Exposure to Substances (SED / MoS Calculations)

SED Calculation Formula

SED (mg/kg bw/day) = (C / 100) × DAa × (DA / 100) / BW

Where: C = ingredient concentration (% w/w)  |  DAa = 7,820 mg/day (entry form daily amount applied)  |  DA = 50% (SCCS dermal absorption default)  |  BW = 70 kg (adult body weight)

Simplifies to: SED = C × 3,910 / 7,000 = C × 0.55857  |  MoS = NOAEL / SED  |  Threshold: MoS ≥ 100

8.10 Fragrance Allergen Assessment

The formulation contains Eucalyptus Globulus Leaf Oil at 0.009% w/w as its sole fragrance ingredient. Eucalyptus oil may contain limonene and other terpene constituents regulated as fragrance allergens under EU Regulation 1223/2009 Annex III (entry 67 for limonene).

For example, even if limonene represented 10% of the essential oil composition (a conservative assumption), its concentration in the finished product would be 0.0009% — below the threshold.

Fragrance Allergen Assessment — No Declaration Required

No other regulated fragrance allergens in Eucalyptus Globulus Leaf Oil are expected to approach the threshold at this use concentration. No allergen declaration is required on the label.

3. Reasoning

3.1 Regulatory Compliance

All 8 ingredients screened against Annexes II–VI of Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009. No prohibited substances (Annex II) identified. No restricted substances above limits (Annex III). Preservatives Sodium Benzoate (0.30% of 0.5% max) and Potassium Sorbate (0.20% of 0.6% max) are within Annex V limits. No Annex IV colorants. No Annex VI UV filters. No CMR 1A/1B substances. No nanomaterials declared.

3.2 Exposure Assessment

Entry form exposure parameters applied: DAa = 7,820 mg/day, 50% dermal absorption (SCCS default), 70 kg body weight, retention factor 1.0. Conservative defaults applied throughout. Actual consumer exposure is likely lower due to individual variation in amount applied and skin surface area.

3.3 Systemic Safety

All MoS values substantially exceed 100. Lowest MoS: 358 (PEG-40 Hydrogenated Castor Oil). Full MoS results: Magnesium Chloride (812), Propanediol (895), PEG-40 HCO (358), Sodium Benzoate (29,837), Potassium Sorbate (2,686), Eucalyptus Globulus Leaf Oil (260,431). Citric Acid at 0.001% is a trace pH adjuster exempt from formal MoS calculation. No ingredients present any systemic safety concern at the calculated leave-on SED levels.

3.4 Local Effects

Skin Sensitisation: Eucalyptus Globulus Leaf Oil is classified Skin Sens. 1 (H317) at bulk. At 0.009% in the finished product, the concentration is well below sensitisation thresholds for leave-on products. All other ingredients are not classified as skin sensitisers at cosmetic use concentrations. No ingredient classified as a respiratory sensitiser.

Skin / Eye Irritation: PEG-40 Hydrogenated Castor Oil and Magnesium Chloride may cause mild irritation on prolonged contact at bulk concentrations; at use levels in this formulation, no irritation concern is identified. Sodium Benzoate, Potassium Sorbate, and Citric Acid are classified Eye Irrit. 2 (H319) at bulk but present no eye irritation concern at the concentrations used in this product. The pH of 4.8–5.5 is compatible with skin and does not create additional irritation risk.

3.5 Microbiological Safety

The product is preserved with Sodium Benzoate (0.30%) and Potassium Sorbate (0.20%), confirmed effective by preservation efficacy challenge testing (ISO 11930, XRAEP, 21/11/2023) achieving Criteria A for all four organisms (complete elimination by Day 7). Finished product microbiological specification meets ISO 17516 Category 2 limits. The spray delivery system limits consumer-side microbial contamination during use.

3.6 Impurities and Packaging

All ingredients declared at 100% purity per supplier SDS. PEG-40 Hydrogenated Castor Oil may carry trace 1,4-dioxane — supplier purification data requested (OI-001). No other impurity concerns identified. HDPE packaging is chemically inert and confirmed compatible with the aqueous formulation; no interaction identified during stability testing.
