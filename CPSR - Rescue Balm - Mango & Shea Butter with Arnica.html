Notes on Dermal Absorption (DAp) Values

DAp Justification by Ingredient Class

† Triglyceride / Plant Oils (DAp = 0.1%): Sunflower Seed Oil, Hydrogenated Coconut Oil, Shea Butter, Grape Seed Oil, Sweet Almond Oil, Mango Seed Butter, and Evening Primrose Oil are predominantly composed of triglycerides with average molecular weights in the range of 800–900 Da. Per the “500 Da rule” (Bos & Meinardi, 2000), molecules exceeding 500 Da do not readily penetrate intact skin. A conservative DAp of 0.1% is applied, consistent with published CIR assessments for fatty vegetable oils and SCCS Notes of Guidance, 11th Revision (SCCS/1628/21), which permit DAp values lower than 100% where physico-chemical data justifies negligible systemic penetration.

※ Tocopherol (DAp = 1%): Tocopherol (MW ~430 Da) is lipophilic; however, its large molecular volume and steric bulk restrict dermal penetration. A conservative DAp of 1% is applied based on published literature values for tocopherol skin absorption.

§ Mentha Piperita Oil (DAp = 25%): Peppermint oil is composed primarily of menthol (MW 156 Da) and menthone (MW 154 Da). These small, lipophilic terpenes penetrate intact skin readily. A DAp of 25% is applied as a conservative estimate based on published dermal absorption data for terpene mixtures.

¶ Botanical Extracts (DAp = 5%): Calendula Officinalis Flower Extract and Arnica Montana Flower Extract are complex mixtures used at low concentrations (0.25% each). A conservative DAp of 5% is applied.

Notes on NOAEL Values

Ingredients with No Formal NOAEL Established

‡ Mangifera Indica (Mango) Seed Butter and Oenothera Biennis (Evening Primrose) Oil do not have a formal oral NOAEL established in literature. Both substances are addressed in the CIR Draft Safety Assessment for plant-derived oils (Morin & Srinivasan, 2022). Their fatty acid profiles (oleic, stearic, linoleic, gamma-linolenic acids) are analogous to well-studied plant oils (e.g., shea butter, sunflower oil). Given the extremely low SED of 0.001 mg/kg bw/day at 0.1% DAp, and considering their long histories of safe use in food and cosmetics, both substances are assessed as safe at the concentrations used in this formulation (1.00% w/w each).

Allergen Declaration

The formulation contains no declared fragrance allergens above the reporting threshold for leave-on products per Annex III of Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009 and EU Regulation 2023/1545. Mentha Piperita Oil is present at 0.30% w/w; any trace allergens (e.g., linalool, limonene) within this fraction are well below the 0.001% declaration threshold in the finished product.

3.2 Risk Classification: LOW MICROBIOLOGICAL RISK

ISO 29621:2017 Classification

Per ISO 29621:2017 (Cosmetics — Microbiology — Guidelines for risk assessment of microbiologically low-risk products), this product is classified as LOW MICROBIOLOGICAL RISK based on:

  1. Fully anhydrous formulation: The product contains no water. All 11 ingredients are oils, butters, waxes, or oil-soluble extracts. Microbial growth requires free water.
  2. Aw < 0.6: Well below the 0.75 threshold below which microbial growth is not supported by any known microorganism.
  3. Tocopherol (antioxidant): Present at 0.50% to prevent oxidative degradation of unsaturated fatty acids.

8.3 Specific Notes: Arnica Montana Flower Extract

Arnica Montana — Regulatory Status and Safety at 0.25%

Arnica Montana Flower Extract is listed in CosIng as a permitted cosmetic ingredient with functions: fragrance, skin conditioning, and perfuming. The primary active component of concern is helenalin, a sesquiterpene lactone. At a product-level concentration of 0.25% extract, the systemic exposure to helenalin-equivalents is negligible (SED = 0.014 mg/kg bw/day at 5% DAp; MoS = 71,619). The ingredient is assessed as safe at the concentrations used in this formulation.

1. Assessment Conclusion

Assessment Conclusion — SAFE
Based on the information provided, the cosmetic product “Rescue Balm — Mango & Shea Butter with Arnica” (Body Balm, 50 g) is considered safe for human health when used under normal or reasonably foreseeable conditions of use, in compliance with the instructions provided for the consumer. GMP evidence (ISO 22716:2007 compliance) is on file. All MoS values for ingredients with established NOAELs exceed 1,000. All ingredients comply with Annexes II, III, V, and VI of Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009. No fragrance allergens are present above the declaration threshold for leave-on products. The product is suitable for adult use as a leave-on body balm.

3. Reasoning

3.1 Regulatory Compliance

All 11 ingredients screened against Annexes II–VI of Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009. No prohibited substances (Annex II) identified. Arnica Montana Flower Extract (Annex III restricted substance) is present at 0.25% w/w, within the permitted concentration limit for leave-on products. No Annex V preservatives or Annex VI UV filters are present or required (anhydrous product). No CMR Category 1A/1B substances identified. The formulation is fully compliant with Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009.

3.2 Exposure Assessment

SCCS exposure scenario applied: 7.82 g/day applied amount, 15,670 cm² skin surface (body + head), RF 1.0 (leave-on), 70 kg body weight. Total product daily exposure: 111.71 mg/kg bw/day. Ingredient-specific DAp values applied based on molecular weight and physicochemical properties, consistent with SCCS Notes of Guidance, 11th Revision. Conservative DAp of 0.1% applied to all triglyceride oils (justified by the 500 Da rule and published CIR data). Higher DAp values applied to terpene-containing oils (peppermint: 25%) and botanical extracts (5%).

3.3 Systemic Safety

All MoS values for ingredients with established NOAELs substantially exceed 1,000. Lowest MoS: Mentha Piperita Oil at 1,194. Ingredients without formal NOAELs (Mango Seed Butter and Evening Primrose Oil) present SED values of 0.001 mg/kg bw/day — negligible systemic exposure. No systemic safety concerns at declared concentrations.

3.4 Local Effects

Sensitisation: Mentha Piperita Oil (0.30%) and Arnica Montana Flower Extract (0.25%) carry theoretical sensitisation potential; both are within safe concentration ranges for leave-on products and no allergen declarations are required above the applicable threshold. Irritation: All ingredients are well-established emollients and conditioners; no irritation potential identified at finished product concentrations. Eye: Occlusive balm near periocular area mitigated by label warning to avoid direct eye contact.

3.5 Microbiological Safety

The product is fully anhydrous; water activity Aw < 0.6, inherently preventing microbial growth. Full microbiological testing (ISO 17516) and challenge testing (ISO 11930, Criterion A) conducted by MySwissLab+ of SBR Group Sarl — all results PASS. Tocopherol provides antioxidant protection to prevent oxidative degradation of unsaturated fatty acids.

3.6 Impurity & Packaging

No prohibited impurities detected. No PBT/vPvB/endocrine-disrupting components ≥ 0.1% identified. Polypropylene (PP) jar confirmed chemically compatible with the anhydrous lipid-based formulation by 12-week stability testing at three temperature conditions (5°C, ambient, 40°C). No leaching or packaging interaction observed.

3.7 Uncertainty & Conservatism

Conservative DAp values applied throughout (0.1% for triglyceride oils; higher values for smaller molecules). Body weight of 70 kg (SCCS default adult). Daily applied amount of 7.82 g is the SCCS reference value for leave-on body products; actual consumer use may be lower. Actual safety margins in real-world use are likely higher than the calculated MoS values presented here.

3.8 Special Populations

Product intended for adult use only. Not marketed for children under 3 years of age. Individuals with known sensitivities to tree nuts (Prunus Amygdalus Dulcis — Sweet Almond) should perform a patch test prior to first use. No specific restrictions apply to pregnant or breastfeeding women based on the toxicological profile of the formulation at the concentrations used.

3.9 Final Justification

Safety assessed by evaluating qualitative/quantitative composition, physico-chemical characteristics, microbiological quality, toxicological profile, exposure levels, and MoS for each ingredient. All MoS values for ingredients with established NOAELs exceed 1,000. The two ingredients without formal NOAELs present negligible systemic exposure (SED = 0.001 mg/kg bw/day). No prohibited or restricted substances exceed their respective limits. The anhydrous formulation is inherently microbiologically safe, confirmed by ISO 17516 and ISO 11930 testing. The product is considered safe for human health when used as directed on the label.
