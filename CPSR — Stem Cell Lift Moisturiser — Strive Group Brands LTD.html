4. Impurities, Traces, and Packaging Material

4.1 Impurity Screening

No ingredients on EU Annex II (prohibited substances) were identified in the formula. All Annex III (restricted) ingredients are used within permitted conditions.

  • Heavy metals: No heavy metal-containing ingredients are present as primary ingredients. Zinc Oxide is used at 1.0% w/w, well within the EU Annex VI permitted limit of 25% w/w. No impurity data indicating elevated heavy metal contamination was submitted.
  • 1,4-Dioxane: No ethoxylated (PEG-derivative) ingredients are present. Risk of 1,4-dioxane contamination is negligible.
  • Formaldehyde: No formaldehyde-releasing preservatives are present. Risk is negligible.
  • Nitrosamines: No secondary amine-containing ingredients that would present a nitrosamine risk were identified.
  • Pesticides / Residual Solvents: Botanical ingredients (oils, butters, extracts) may contain trace residual impurities, assumed to be within acceptable limits per standard cosmetic-grade specifications.

Fragrance Allergens: No allergens above 0.01% leave-on declaration threshold identified.

3.2 Exposure Assessment

SCCS category: Face cream (leave-on), Table 3A, SCCS/1647/22. DAa: 24.5 mg/kg bw/day (conservative SCCS face cream default). Body weight: 60 kg (SCCS adult default). Retention factor: 1.0 (leave-on product). DA defaults: 50% small-molecule ingredients; 10% high MW lipids; 1% ZnO and high MW polysaccharides. No children’s or vulnerable population adjustments required (adult-only product). No inhalation exposure pathway — cream format, not a spray.

All MoS values ≥ 100. Key values: Lowest MoS: Allantoin at 0.100% — MoS = 11,592 (PASS, threshold 100). Camellia Sinensis Leaf Extract at 0.050% — MoS = 32,653 (PASS). Ocular Safety: Not an eye product; foreseeable eye contact addressed by label warning. Non-irritant formulation confirmed by MSDS. Water activity >0.75 (aqueous emulsion) — preserved system required and in place. ISO 11930 Criteria A achieved for all 4 organisms (P. aeruginosa, S. aureus, C. albicans, A. brasiliensis) at Days 7, 14, and 28. ISO 17516:2004 microbiological quality: all parameters compliant.
